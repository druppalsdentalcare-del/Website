Infection control

Your safety is non-negotiable

We follow a strict, documented sterilisation protocol for every single patient — the same standard we would expect for our own families.

  1. 1Instruments are cleaned in an ultrasonic cleaner to remove debris.
  2. 2They are sealed in sterilisation pouches with colour-change indicators.
  3. 3Pouches are sterilised in a Class-B vacuum autoclave.
  4. 4Pouches are opened in front of you, at your appointment.
  5. 5Needles, gloves, masks, suction tips and bibs are single-use.
  6. 6Surfaces and the dental chair are disinfected between patients.
