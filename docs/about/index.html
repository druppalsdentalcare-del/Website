About the clinic

Careful planning, time with patients, lasting results

Dr. Uppal's Dental Hub & Implant Center is a private dental practice in New Delhi, focused on dental implants, smile design and unhurried care for the whole family.

U
Dr. UppalBDS, MDSFounder & Lead Dentist
Founder & lead dentist

Dr. Uppal, BDS, MDS

Dr. Uppal founded Dr. Uppal's Dental Hub & Implant Center to offer the kind of dentistry they would want for their own family: unhurried, honest and planned in detail. The clinical focus is dental implants and full-mouth rehabilitation, alongside cosmetic and general dentistry.

Every treatment plan is explained in plain language, with the options, the trade-offs and the costs set out before anything begins.

What we believe

Four promises we never bend

Plan first

No drilling before diagnosis. Good results start with good records and a clear plan.

Keep what you can

Be clear about cost

Written, itemised estimates, and no treatment you have not agreed to.

Make it comfortable

Safety & sterilisation

Your safety is never hurried

Instruments are cleaned, sealed and sterilised for every patient, and surfaces are disinfected between appointments.

  1. 1Instruments cleaned in an ultrasonic bath to remove debris
  2. 2Sealed in sterilisation pouches with colour-change indicators
  3. 3Sterilised in a Class B vacuum autoclave
  4. 4Pouches opened in front of you at your appointment
  5. 5Single-use needles, gloves, bibs and suction tips
  6. 6Digital X-rays with lower radiation than film
