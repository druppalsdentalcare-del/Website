Patient guide

What to expect, every step of the way

Before, during and after your visit — so you can walk in feeling informed, calm and in control.

  1. 01

    Before your first visit

    • Arrive ten minutes early to complete a short medical history
    • Bring a list of medicines you take, and any recent dental X-rays
    • Tell us about diabetes, heart conditions, blood thinners, allergies or pregnancy
    • Eat normally unless we tell you otherwise
    • Nervous? Tell us when you book — we’ll plan a gentler, slower visit
  2. 02

    At your consultation

    • A conversation about what bothers you and what you want
    • Examination of teeth, gums, bite and soft tissues
    • Digital X-rays or a 3D scan, only if needed
    • Your options explained with timelines and costs
    • A written, itemised plan — nothing starts without your agreement
  3. 03

    During treatment

    • Effective local anaesthesia — you feel pressure, not pain
    • A ‘stop’ signal you can use at any time
    • Sterile instruments opened in front of you
    • We explain each step as we go
    • Fixed dates for crowns, bridges and lab work — and we keep them
  4. 04

    After treatment

    • Written aftercare instructions for your procedure
    • A follow-up call or review appointment where needed
    • Reminders for check-ups and cleanings
    • Yearly reviews for implants and large restorations
    • Call us straight away for swelling that keeps increasing, bleeding that won’t stop with pressure, or a fever

Payment

We share an itemised estimate before treatment, so you know the full cost up front. Ask our front desk about accepted payment methods.

Ask us about staged payments for longer treatment plans.

Insurance

Most health policies cover dental treatment only after an accident or when hospitalisation is needed. If your policy or employer includes OPD dental cover, we provide the bills and reports you need.

Common questions

Straight answers before you visit

The questions patients ask us most. There are many more in our FAQ section.

All FAQs
How do I book an appointment?

Use the Book a consultation button on any page, call us, or message us on WhatsApp. Choose your treatment and preferred time and our team will confirm your slot.

What happens at the first visit?

We talk through your concerns, examine your teeth and gums, take X-rays or a 3D scan if needed, and explain your options with timelines and costs. Nothing is started without your agreement.

What should I bring to my first visit?

Bring a list of medicines you take, any previous dental X-rays or reports, and details of medical conditions such as diabetes, heart problems, pregnancy or allergies.

Do you treat dental emergencies?

Yes. We keep priority slots every day for severe toothache, swelling, broken or knocked-out teeth. Call or WhatsApp us and we'll see you as soon as possible.

Do you treat children and older patients?

Yes. We see the whole family, from a child's first check-up to dentures and implants for older patients.

How much do dental implants cost in Delhi?

The cost depends on how many teeth are replaced, the implant system, whether bone grafting is needed and the type of crown. We give you a written, itemised estimate after your consultation and scan, before any treatment starts.

How much will my treatment cost?

Cost depends on the treatment, the number of teeth, materials chosen (for example zirconia vs metal-ceramic crowns, or implant brand) and case complexity. After your examination we give you a written treatment plan with transparent costs before starting anything.
