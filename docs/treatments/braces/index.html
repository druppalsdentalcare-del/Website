Braces (Metal & Ceramic) in New Delhi

Braces use brackets bonded to the teeth and a connecting wire to move teeth gradually into the correct position. Treatment typically takes 12–24 months with adjustments every 4–6 weeks. Options include metal, tooth-coloured ceramic and self-ligating braces, and they work at any age.

What is Braces?

Straight teeth are not just about looks. Properly aligned teeth are easier to clean, wear more evenly and put less strain on your jaw joints.

Braces can correct crowding, gaps, protruding teeth, deep bites, open bites, underbites and crossbites — in children, teenagers and adults alike.

Signs you may need it

  • Crooked or crowded teeth
  • Gaps between teeth
  • Protruding front teeth
  • Upper and lower teeth don't meet properly
  • Difficulty biting or chewing

The procedure, step by step

  1. 1

    Orthodontic records

    Photos, X-rays and scans to analyse teeth and jaw relationships.

  2. 2

    Treatment plan

    We explain the movements, options and expected timeline.

  3. 3

    Bonding braces

    Brackets are bonded and the wire is placed — painless, about an hour.

  4. 4

    Adjustments

    Regular visits every 4–6 weeks to progress tooth movement.

  5. 5

    Retention

    Braces are removed and retainers keep teeth in their new position.

Benefits

Straighter, healthier smile
Improved bite and chewing
Easier cleaning and lower decay risk
Effective for complex cases

Recovery & aftercare

  • Avoid hard and sticky foods (nuts, chikki, toffees, popcorn)
  • Brush carefully around brackets after every meal
  • Wear retainers exactly as instructed

Braces: frequently asked questions

Is there an age limit for braces?

No. While treatment is often started between 10 and 14, healthy adults of any age can have braces. Adult treatment is very common today.

Do braces hurt?

Getting braces fitted doesn't hurt. Teeth may feel sore for 3–5 days after placement and after adjustments; soft food and simple painkillers help. Wax can soothe any rubbing on the cheeks.

Metal or ceramic braces — which should I choose?

Metal braces are the most economical and robust. Ceramic braces are tooth-coloured and much less noticeable, but are slightly more expensive and need a little more care.

Why do I need to wear a retainer?

Teeth naturally tend to move back towards their old positions. Retainers hold them in place while bone and gums stabilise, and long-term night-time wear is usually recommended.

How much does it cost?

The cost of braces (metal & ceramic) depends on your diagnosis, the number of teeth involved and the materials chosen. After your examination you’ll receive a clear, itemised estimate before treatment starts. Book a consultation to get your personalised plan.
