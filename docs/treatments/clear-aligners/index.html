Clear Aligners in New Delhi

Clear aligners are a series of custom, transparent, removable trays that gradually straighten teeth. They must be worn 20–22 hours a day, are changed every 1–2 weeks, and treatment typically takes 6–18 months. They are suitable for many mild to moderate cases of crowding, spacing and bite issues.

What is Clear Aligners?

Clear aligners let you straighten your teeth discreetly. Because they are removable, you can eat what you like, brush and floss normally and take them out for important moments.

Using a 3D scan of your teeth, we plan every tooth movement digitally and show you a simulation of your final result before you start.

Signs you may need it

  • Mild to moderate crowding
  • Gaps between teeth
  • Teeth that shifted after braces
  • Want a discreet option for work or social life

The procedure, step by step

  1. 1

    3D scan

    A digital scan replaces messy impressions.

  2. 2

    Digital simulation

    See your planned tooth movements and final smile on screen.

  3. 3

    Attachments

    Small tooth-coloured bumps may be bonded to help aligners grip.

  4. 4

    Aligner series

    Switch to a new set every 1–2 weeks with periodic check-ins.

  5. 5

    Retention

    Retainers maintain your new smile.

Benefits

Nearly invisible
Removable — no food restrictions
Comfortable, no wires
Fewer clinic visits

Recovery & aftercare

  • Wear aligners 20–22 hours daily
  • Remove them to eat or drink anything except plain water
  • Brush before putting them back in
  • Clean aligners with cool water — never hot

Clear Aligners: frequently asked questions

Are clear aligners as effective as braces?

For many mild to moderate cases, yes. Complex cases involving large tooth movements or significant jaw discrepancies may be treated more predictably with braces. Your dentist will advise after assessment.

Can I eat with aligners on?

No. Remove aligners to eat and drink anything other than plain water, then brush before putting them back. This prevents staining and trapping food against your teeth.

Do clear aligners hurt?

You may feel pressure for a day or two when switching to a new set — a sign that they're working. Most people find aligners more comfortable than braces.

What happens if I don't wear my aligners enough?

Teeth won't move as planned and aligners may stop fitting, which can extend treatment or require new aligners. Consistent wear of 20–22 hours a day is essential.

How much does it cost?

The cost of clear aligners depends on your diagnosis, the number of teeth involved and the materials chosen. After your examination you’ll receive a clear, itemised estimate before treatment starts. Book a consultation to get your personalised plan.
