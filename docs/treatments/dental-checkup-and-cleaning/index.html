What is Dental Check-up & Cleaning?

Regular check-ups are the single most effective way to avoid painful and expensive dental problems. During your visit we examine every tooth, your gums, bite, jaw joints and soft tissues, and take low-dose digital X-rays when needed to see what the eye cannot.

Professional cleaning (scaling and polishing) removes hardened tartar that brushing and flossing cannot. It keeps gums healthy, freshens breath and lifts surface stains from tea, coffee and tobacco.

Signs you may need it

  • It has been more than 6 months since your last visit
  • Bleeding gums while brushing
  • Bad breath that does not go away
  • Visible yellow or brown deposits near the gumline
  • Sensitivity to hot or cold

The procedure, step by step

  1. 1

    Consultation

    We discuss your concerns, medical history and any medicines you take.

  2. 2

    Examination & X-rays

    A complete oral examination, plus digital X-rays where indicated.

  3. 3

    Ultrasonic scaling

    Gentle vibrations and a water spray loosen and wash away tartar.

  4. 4

    Polishing

    Teeth are polished smooth so plaque finds it harder to stick.

  5. 5

    Personal care plan

    You receive brushing and flossing tips, plus a plan for any treatment needed.

Benefits

Catches cavities while they are small and cheap to fix
Prevents gum disease and tooth loss
Fresher breath and a brighter smile
Oral cancer screening at every visit

Recovery & aftercare

  • Mild sensitivity for a day is normal
  • Avoid strongly coloured food and drinks for a few hours after polishing
  • Brush twice daily with fluoride toothpaste and floss once a day

Dental Check-up & Cleaning: frequently asked questions

How often should I get my teeth cleaned?

For most people every 6 months. If you have gum disease, diabetes, braces or a history of heavy tartar, your dentist may recommend every 3–4 months.

Does teeth scaling damage enamel or loosen teeth?

No. Scaling removes only tartar, not enamel. Teeth can feel slightly 'loose' or sensitive afterwards because the tartar that was bridging them is gone and gums are healing — this settles within days as gums tighten.

Is dental cleaning painful?

Most patients feel only vibration and water spray. If your gums are inflamed or you have sensitivity, we can use a numbing gel or local anaesthetic to keep you comfortable.

Are dental X-rays safe?

Yes. Digital dental X-rays use a very low dose of radiation — roughly comparable to a few hours of natural background radiation. We take them only when clinically needed.

Why do my gums bleed when I brush?

Bleeding gums are usually an early sign of gingivitis caused by plaque build-up. A professional cleaning and better home care typically resolve it within 1–2 weeks.

How much does it cost?

The cost of dental check-up & cleaning depends on your diagnosis, the number of teeth involved and the materials chosen. After your examination you’ll receive a clear, itemised estimate before treatment starts. Book a consultation to get your personalised plan.
