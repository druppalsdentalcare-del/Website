Crowns & Bridges

A dental crown is a custom cap that covers a damaged or root-canal-treated tooth to restore its strength and appearance; a bridge replaces one or more missing teeth by anchoring false teeth to crowns on the neighbouring teeth. Treatment usually takes 2 visits and modern zirconia or ceramic crowns can last 10–15 years or more.

What is Crowns & Bridges?

Crowns protect and strengthen teeth weakened by large fillings, cracks or root canal treatment, and can transform the shape and colour of discoloured or misshapen teeth.

We offer metal-free zirconia and lithium-disilicate (E-max) crowns that are strong, biocompatible and look like natural enamel, as well as porcelain-fused-to-metal (PFM) options.

Signs you may need it

  • A tooth after root canal treatment
  • A cracked or heavily filled tooth
  • A broken or worn-down tooth
  • One or more missing teeth
  • Discoloured or misshapen teeth

The procedure, step by step

  1. 1

    Preparation

    The tooth is numbed and gently shaped to make room for the crown.

  2. 2

    Impressions / scan

    Precise impressions or digital scans are taken and the shade is selected.

  3. 3

    Temporary crown

    A temporary crown protects the tooth while the final one is crafted in the lab.

  4. 4

    Try-in

    We check fit, bite and colour with you before cementing.

  5. 5

    Cementation

    The final crown or bridge is permanently bonded in place.

Benefits

Restores full chewing strength
Protects weak teeth from fracture
Natural, lifelike appearance
Fixed — no removing at night

Recovery & aftercare

  • Brush and floss around the crown like a natural tooth
  • Use a floss threader or water flosser under bridges
  • Avoid biting very hard objects like ice or bones

Crowns & Bridges: frequently asked questions

Which crown is best — zirconia, E-max or metal-ceramic?

Zirconia is extremely strong and ideal for back teeth and bridges. E-max offers the best translucency for front teeth. Metal-ceramic (PFM) is economical and durable but may show a grey line at the gum over time. Your dentist will recommend the best option for each tooth.

How long do dental crowns last?

With good oral hygiene and regular check-ups, crowns commonly last 10–15 years, and many last much longer.

Crown or implant — what is better for a missing tooth?

A bridge requires trimming the adjacent teeth to support it, whereas an implant replaces the tooth independently and preserves bone. Implants are often preferred when the neighbouring teeth are healthy; bridges are a good option when those teeth already need crowns or implants aren't suitable.

Does getting a crown hurt?

No. Tooth preparation is done under local anaesthesia. Some sensitivity for a few days after the temporary crown is normal.

How much does it cost?

The cost of crowns & bridges depends on your diagnosis, the number of teeth involved and the materials chosen. After your examination you'll receive a clear, written estimate before treatment starts. Book a consultation to get your personalised plan.
