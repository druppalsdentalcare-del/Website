Dentures in New Delhi

Dentures are removable replacements for missing teeth. Complete dentures replace all teeth in an arch; partial dentures fill gaps between remaining teeth. Modern options include lightweight flexible dentures and implant-supported overdentures that snap in securely. Making a denture usually takes 4–5 visits.

What is Dentures?

Well-made dentures restore your ability to chew, speak clearly and smile confidently, and support your lips and cheeks so your face doesn't look sunken.

We craft dentures with natural-looking teeth and gum-coloured bases, carefully recording your bite and facial proportions so they fit comfortably and look like you.

Signs you may need it

  • Several or all teeth missing
  • Old dentures that are loose, broken or painful
  • Difficulty chewing
  • Sunken facial appearance

The procedure, step by step

  1. 1

    Examination

    We assess your gums, bone and any remaining teeth.

  2. 2

    Impressions

    Primary and final impressions record the exact shape of your mouth.

  3. 3

    Bite registration

    Jaw relationship, lip support and tooth shade are recorded.

  4. 4

    Trial denture

    You preview the look and fit in wax before the final denture is made.

  5. 5

    Delivery & adjustments

    The final denture is fitted and adjusted at follow-up visits for comfort.

Recovery & aftercare

  • Remove and clean dentures daily
  • Take them out at night to rest your gums
  • Store in water to prevent warping
  • Have them checked yearly — gums change shape over time

Dentures: frequently asked questions

How long does it take to get used to new dentures?

Most people adapt within 2–4 weeks. Start with soft food cut into small pieces, chew on both sides, and read aloud to practise speaking. Sore spots are common at first and are easily adjusted at the clinic.

Can loose dentures be fixed?

Yes. A loose denture can be relined to fit your gums better. For the most secure fit, 2–4 implants can be placed so the denture snaps on firmly (implant-supported overdenture).

Should I sleep with my dentures in?

It is best to remove dentures at night. This lets your gums rest and reduces the risk of fungal infections and bad breath.

What are flexible dentures?

Flexible dentures are made from a thin, bendable nylon-based material without metal clasps. They are lightweight, comfortable and aesthetic, and are a popular choice for partial dentures.

How much does it cost?

The cost of dentures depends on your diagnosis, the number of teeth involved and the materials chosen. After your examination you’ll receive a clear, itemised estimate before treatment starts. Book a consultation to get your personalised plan.
