Full Mouth Rehabilitation in New Delhi

Full mouth rehabilitation is a planned combination of treatments — such as crowns, implants, root canal treatment, gum therapy and bite correction — that rebuilds all or most of the teeth in both jaws. It is used for severely worn, broken or missing teeth and typically takes several weeks to a few months.

What is Full Mouth Rehabilitation?

When many teeth are affected — from years of grinding, acid erosion, old failing dental work or multiple missing teeth — fixing one tooth at a time rarely works. Full mouth rehabilitation looks at your teeth, gums, bite and jaw joints as one system.

We use digital planning and trial restorations (mock-ups) so you can preview and test the new bite and smile before anything is made final.

Signs you may need it

  • Severely worn or short teeth
  • Many broken, decayed or missing teeth
  • Jaw pain, headaches or clicking from bite problems
  • Multiple failing old crowns and fillings

The procedure, step by step

  1. 1

    Comprehensive diagnosis

    Photos, X-rays, CBCT, scans and bite analysis.

  2. 2

    Treatment plan

    A phased plan with options, timelines and costs explained clearly.

  3. 3

    Foundation

    Infection control, gum treatment, root canals and extractions are completed first.

  4. 4

    Trial restorations

    Temporary teeth test the new bite, comfort and appearance.

  5. 5

    Final restorations

    Definitive crowns, bridges or implant teeth are placed.

Benefits

Eat comfortably again
Relief from bite-related jaw pain
A complete, natural-looking smile
Predictable, planned results

Recovery & aftercare

  • Wear a night guard if you grind your teeth
  • Regular hygiene visits every 3–6 months
  • Avoid using teeth as tools

Full Mouth Rehabilitation: frequently asked questions

How long does full mouth rehabilitation take?

It depends on what is needed. Cases without implants may be completed in a few weeks; cases with implants or gum surgery can take 3–6 months because bone and gums need time to heal.

Is full mouth rehabilitation painful?

Each procedure is carried out under local anaesthesia, and sedation can be discussed for anxious patients. Treatment is spread over appointments to keep each visit comfortable.

Can I get full mouth rehabilitation if I have no teeth?

Yes. For people with no teeth, implant-supported fixed teeth (such as All-on-4 or All-on-6) or implant-retained dentures can rebuild the full mouth.

How much does it cost?

The cost of full mouth rehabilitation depends on your diagnosis, the number of teeth involved and the materials chosen. After your examination you’ll receive a clear, itemised estimate before treatment starts. Book a consultation to get your personalised plan.
