Smile Makeover & Smile Design

A smile makeover is a personalised cosmetic plan that combines treatments — such as whitening, veneers, crowns, gum contouring and aligners — to improve the overall look of your smile. Digital Smile Design lets you preview the result before treatment begins.

What is Smile Makeover & Smile Design?

Every face is different, so every smile makeover should be too. We study your facial proportions, lip line, tooth shapes and gum levels to design a smile that looks naturally yours — just at its best.

With Digital Smile Design and a trial 'mock-up' placed directly on your teeth, you can see, feel and approve your new smile before committing to treatment.

Signs you may need it

  • You hide your smile in photos
  • Uneven, stained or chipped teeth
  • A 'gummy' smile
  • Gaps or mild crowding
  • Preparing for a wedding or milestone

The procedure, step by step

Smile Makeover & Smile Design: frequently asked questions

What is Digital Smile Design (DSD)?

Digital Smile Design uses photos, videos and software to plan your new smile based on your facial features. It helps you and your dentist agree on the result before treatment, making outcomes more predictable.

How long does a smile makeover take?

Simple makeovers using whitening and composite bonding can be completed in about a week. Makeovers with porcelain veneers take 2–3 weeks; those including aligners can take several months.

Can I get a smile makeover before my wedding?

Yes — start planning 3–6 months ahead if possible. This allows time for any alignment or gum treatment, and for veneers or whitening to be finalised well before photos.

How much does it cost?

The cost of smile makeover & smile design depends on your diagnosis, the number of teeth involved and the materials chosen. After your examination you'll receive a clear, written estimate before treatment starts. Book a consultation to get your personalised plan.
